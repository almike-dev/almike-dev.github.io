Privacy Policy – mika-service

mika-service is a private assistant used exclusively by the owner of the connected Google account.

  • It accesses only Gmail, Google Drive and Google Calendar of the owner's own account, after consent via Google OAuth.
  • Data is processed solely to carry out tasks requested by the owner.
  • No data is sold or shared with third parties; no advertising, no tracking.
  • Access can be revoked at any time at https://myaccount.google.com/permissions.

mika-service's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
